# GuildIt — Game Rules: Leveling & Reclass

## Leveling

- Level comes entirely from your **total points** — never stored, always calculated fresh.
- Levels 1–10 are hand-tuned and get harder each step. After level 10, every level just costs **+500 more points** than the last, forever.
- Leveling up is **prestige only** — no power boost. A level 30 player earns the same points per action as a level 1 player.

| Level | Points Needed     |
| ----- | ----------------- |
| 1     | 0                 |
| 2     | 100               |
| 5     | 850               |
| 10    | 4,500             |
| 10+N  | 4,500 + (N × 500) |

## How You Earn Points

Every point in GuildIt traces back to something real you actually did on Reddit. No fake busywork.
| Source | How It Works | Payout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Passive (Automatic)** | Every post, comment, or supportive interaction you make on Reddit contributes points directly to your total. It is instant, automatic, and requires no extra clicks. | Small but constant rewards. Your class grants a **×1.5 bonus** on passive events that match its theme (e.g., Rangers earn bonus points for discovery-style interactions). |
| **Solo Trial (Daily)** | Each day, you face a class-themed question. A Ranger might spot a hidden pattern; a Mender might identify the kindest reply. You cannot cheat—the correct answer is verified only on the server. | **Full points** for correct answers and **partial points** for incorrect answers. Participation is rewarded, not only perfection. |
| **Verified (Retroactive)** | Reddit's backend confirms when your content reaches a meaningful milestone (significant upvotes, reply chains, or saves). | Awarded automatically, sometimes days later. These are your **"legacy points"**—proof that your contribution had lasting impact. |
| **Duels** _(Not finalized)_ | Challenge another player to an asynchronous card battle. Win or lose, you earn points based on your performance. Better strategies earn more rewards, but even a close loss provides some points. | **Win:** significant points<br>**Loss:** small consolation points<br>**Draw:** medium split<br><br>Exact formula TBD. |
| **Guild Raids** _(Not finalized)_ | Coordinate with your full guild (**all 4 classes required**) to complete a massive weekly challenge. Each member contributes through real Reddit actions or shared in-app objectives. | **Participation:** base points for contributing<br>**Completion:** large bonus if the guild succeeds<br>**Full House Bonus:** extra multiplier if all 4 classes participate<br><br>Exact scoring TBD. |

Your class (**Ranger, Mender, Warder, or Weaver**) receives a **×1.5 bonus** on passive events that match its theme.

## Reclassing (Changing Class)

- Costs a **% of your current points** — the "tax."
- **Free at level 1** (grace period for new players).
- Tax starts at **20%** at level 2, **+2% per level**, capped at **40%**.
- You always see the exact cost _before_ confirming — never a surprise.

| Level | Tax       |
| ----- | --------- |
| 1     | Free      |
| 2     | 20%       |
| 5     | 26%       |
| 10    | 36%       |
| 12+   | 40% (max) |

- Losing points from the tax can drop your level too — that's intentional, it's part of the cost.
- No cooldown — you can reclass as often as you want, but the tax makes doing it often expensive on its own.

## Reclassing & Your Guild

- Guild score depends on having all 4 classes represented (**completeness**).
- Reclassing can **complete** your guild (great) or **break** its completeness (costly) — recalculated instantly, automatically.
